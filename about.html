THE FOUNDER

A Story Built on
Family & Craftsmanship

My story begins in Honduras. I came to the United States when I was just 14 years old. From a young age, I learned that hard work, perseverance, and staying true to your values are what help you build a better life. I’ve been working hard ever since, always striving to create a better future for myself and my family.

My passion for carpentry started with my grandfather and my father. Watching them work with their hands, build things from the ground up, and take pride in what they created inspired me to follow the same path. What started as something I learned from my family eventually became a passion of my own.

Over the years, I’ve had the opportunity to work on many different projects, but what has always mattered most to me is doing the job right. I believe in honest communication, quality craftsmanship, and treating every home as if it were my own.

Today, through Andino Home Solutions, I get to do what I love while carrying the values my family taught me. For me, this business is more than building and remodeling homes. It’s about honoring where I came from, providing for my family, and building something I can be proud of.

Gerald Andino
OWNER | ANDINO HOME SOLUTIONS
